About

I build reproducible, Linux-based omics pipelines and apply genomics, transcriptomics and metagenomics to study evolution, immunity, host–microbe interactions and adaptation in aquatic and other organisms. My work emphasises integrative, cross-study analysis of phenotypic and genotypic data to uncover conserved molecular signatures and biomarkers.

I teach and supervise graduate and undergraduate research in fish health, immunology, parasitology and bioinformatics.

Research

Research areas

  • Comparative and evolutionary genomics — fish, bacterial and mitochondrial genomes; pangenomics and phylogenetics
  • Transcriptomics — bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, cross-study meta-analysis
  • Metagenomics and microbiome — 16S rRNA amplicon and shotgun profiling of host-associated communities
  • Epigenomics — whole-genome bisulfite DNA-methylation meta-analysis
  • Fish immunology and host–microbe interaction — immune gene families, bacterial and parasitic pathogens
  • Immunoinformatics and drug design — in-silico vaccine design and natural-compound inhibitors against aquaculture pathogens

Bioinformatics skills

  • Programming and workflow development in Python, R and Bash; Nextflow pipelines; Git/GitHub, Conda, Pixi and Docker; Linux HPC and cloud platforms (AWS, Google Cloud)
  • Genomics, variant and bacterial genome analysis: read QC and mapping, variant and structural-variant calling (GATK, FreeBayes, Manta, CNVkit), annotation (SnpEff, VEP), genome assembly and AMR profiling (SPAdes, Unicycler, Prokka, ABRicate, CARD), MLST, and mitochondrial genome assembly (GetOrganelle, MITOS2)
  • Transcriptomics and epigenomics: bulk and single-cell RNA-seq (STAR, HISAT2, Salmon, DESeq2, edgeR, Seurat, Scanpy), miRNA profiling, bisulfite-seq methylation and DMR analysis (Bismark, methylKit, DSS), and ChIP-seq
  • Metagenomics and eDNA: amplicon and shotgun profiling (QIIME2, DADA2, VSEARCH, Kraken2, Bracken, MetaPhlAn, HUMAnN), and diversity and functional analysis (phyloseq, PICRUSt2, MEGAN)
  • Proteomics and structural bioinformatics: homology modelling and visualization (SWISS-MODEL, PyMOL, Discovery Studio), docking and virtual screening (PyRx, AutoDock Vina), pocket and ADME analysis (CASTp, SwissADME), and molecular dynamics (GROMACS)
  • Population and quantitative genomics: GWAS (PLINK, TASSEL, GAPIT), FST and haplotype diversity, and QTL mapping and linkage analysis (MapQTL, QTL Cartographer); geospatial mapping with QGIS and ArcGIS

Molecular lab work

  • Experimental design, setup, management of laboratory workflows and biological studies
  • DNA extraction, PCR amplification, gel electrophoresis documentation and sequence library preparation
  • RNA extraction, cDNA synthesis, RT-PCR, and qPCR quantification and sequence library preparation
  • Bacterial culture, biochemical characterization, antibiogram testing, and molecular identification
  • Fish parasite morphometric identification, permanent slide preparation, and molecular identification
