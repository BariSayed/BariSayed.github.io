About

My research integrates molecular biology, genomics, and bioinformatics to investigate evolution, immune regulation, host–microbe interactions, and environmental adaptation in aquatic organisms. I have experience analyzing chromosome-level fish genomes, bacterial whole genomes, mitochondrial genomes, bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, amplicon and shotgun metagenomics, and eDNA datasets. My work focuses on identifying genomic signatures, molecular biomarkers, microbial community patterns, and regulatory pathways associated with immunity, adaptation, and evolutionary change in aquatic systems.

  • Metagenomics (16S rRNA amplicon & shotgun) and microbiome profiling
  • Fish immunology, immune regulation & host–microbe interactions
  • Immunoinformatics & computer-aided drug design
