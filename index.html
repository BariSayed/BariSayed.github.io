About

Research Interest

My research integrates molecular biology wet-lab techniques, including DNA/RNA extraction, PCR/qPCR, cDNA synthesis, sequencing library preparation, and bacterial culture and identification with computational dry-lab bioinformatic approaches encompassing genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and epigenomics data analysis. By combining phenotypic and genotypic data, I investigate evolutionary dynamics, immune regulation, host–microbe interactions, and environmental adaptation. I have hands-on experience analyzing chromosome-level genome assemblies, short- and long-read bacterial genomes, bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, DNA methylation, shotgun metagenomics, and eDNA datasets. Through these approaches, I identify genomic signatures, molecular biomarkers, microbial community patterns, and regulatory pathways underlying adaptation and evolutionary change. I have authored more than twenty peer-reviewed articles, many as corresponding author, serve as a reviewer for several international journals, and mentor graduate and undergraduate students. I am a member of several international scientific societies.

Research

Research Themes

Comparative & Evolutionary Genomics

Chromosome-level fish genomes, bacterial whole genomes and complete mitochondrial genomes to resolve lineage, phylogeny and genomic signatures of adaptation.

Transcriptomics & Meta-analysis

Integrative meta-analysis of bulk and single-cell RNA-seq data to identify conserved stress- and injury-response programs across studies and species.

Microbiome & Metagenomics

16S rRNA amplicon and shotgun metagenomic profiling of host-associated and environmental microbial communities, including antibiotic-perturbed gut microbiomes.

Fish Immunology & Host–Microbe Interaction

Characterising immune gene families and host responses to bacterial and parasitic pathogens in cultured and wild fish.

Immunoinformatics & Drug Design

In-silico vaccine design and computer-aided discovery of natural-compound inhibitors against aquaculture pathogens.

Fish Health & Sustainable Aquaculture

Molecular diagnosis of fish disease, antimicrobial resistance surveillance and evidence for sustainable aquaculture systems.
