About

Research Interest

I work at the interface of molecular wet-lab biology and computational genomics, studying how aquatic animals respond to disease, stress and a changing environment.

QuestionsEvolution, immune regulation, host–microbe interactions and environmental adaptation
OmicsChromosome-level genome assembly, short- & long-read bacterial genomes, bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, DNA methylation, shotgun metagenomics and eDNA
Wet labDNA/RNA extraction, PCR/qPCR, cDNA synthesis, sequencing library preparation, bacterial culture and identification
OutputsGenomic signatures, molecular biomarkers, microbial community patterns and regulatory pathways of adaptation

Published research

  1. Bulk RNA-Seq Meta-Analysis Pipeline (Omyk_Stress_MetaRNASeq). RNA-seq preprocessing and meta-analysis scripts for heat- and hypoxia-stress responses in rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss). Paper: BMC Genomics 2025. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/Omyk_Stress_MetaRNASeq
  2. Rainbow Trout Stress Transcriptomic Meta-Analysis (bigbiolab/Omyk_Transcriptomic_Meta). Analysis repository (with Big Bioinformatics Lab) for the published meta-analysis of heat and hypoxia stress in rainbow trout. Paper: BMC Genomics 2025. Public
    https://github.com/bigbiolab/Omyk_Transcriptomic_Meta
  3. Shotgun Metagenomics Pipeline: Mystus cavasius Gut (mystus-cavasius-gut-shotgun-metagenomics). Reproducible shotgun metagenomics pipeline and R analyses for the gut microbiome of oxytetracycline-treated Mystus cavasius. Paper: Antonie van Leeuwenhoek 2025. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/mystus-cavasius-gut-shotgun-metagenomics
  4. Aeromonas diversa Whole-Genome Sequence (aeromonas-diversa-tilapia-wgs). Whole-genome sequence of Aeromonas diversa SAU1 isolated from tilapia (Illumina MiSeq), with antimicrobial-resistance and virulence profiling. Paper: Data in Brief 2025. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/aeromonas-diversa-tilapia-wgs

Methods and reproducible pipelines

  1. Aeromonas PacBio HiFi Genome Assembly (aeromonas-hifi-assembly). PacBio HiFi whole-genome assembly of Aeromonas (A. dhakensis) with Flye and hifiasm — single circular chromosome, benchmarked against a CLR draft; public data SRR30596866. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/aeromonas-hifi-assembly
  2. Aeromonas hydrophila Nanopore WGS (aeromonas-hydrophila-nanopore-wgs). Oxford Nanopore long-read genome assembly and annotation of Aeromonas hydrophila with nf-core/bacass (Nextflow/Apptainer); public data SRR38923685. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/aeromonas-hydrophila-nanopore-wgs
  3. Aeromonas hydrophila PacBio WGS (aeromonas-hydrophila-pacbio-wgs). PacBio long-read genome assembly and annotation of Aeromonas hydrophila with nf-core/bacass (Nextflow/Apptainer); public data DRR153381. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/aeromonas-hydrophila-pacbio-wgs
  4. Long-read Metagenome-to-MAG Pipeline (longread-metagenome-mag). Reproducible Nextflow (DSL2) long-read metagenome-to-MAG pipeline (PacBio/Nanopore): assembly, binning, MAG QC, taxonomy, function and AMR, with a worked example on public data. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/longread-metagenome-mag

Training workflows

    https://github.com/BariSayed/gwas-data-analysis
  2. miRNA Differential Expression Analysis (mirna-deg-analysis). Human miRNA-seq differential expression, target mapping and KEGG enrichment; training assignment, code only, data belong to the course instructor. Private
    https://github.com/BariSayed/mirna-deg-analysis
  3. Bacterial WGS with Illumina and Nanopore (wgs-nanopore-bacterial). Bacterial whole-genome workflow combining Illumina and Oxford Nanopore reads: assembly, variant calling, structural variants and annotation; completed training assignment. Public
    https://github.com/BariSayed/wgs-nanopore-bacterial
  4. Whole-Exome Variant Calling (wes-variant-calling). Whole-exome sequencing variant-calling pipeline (E. coli, SRR27293200); completed training assignment. Private
    https://github.com/BariSayed/wes-variant-calling
  5. eDNA Fish Metabarcoding, Downstream Analysis (edna-fish-metabarcoding). Downstream OTU-table analysis code from an eDNA fish-metabarcoding training workshop; code only. The sequencing data, feature tables and pipeline belong to the workshop host (a visiting professor) and are not redistributed. Private
    https://github.com/BariSayed/edna-fish-metabarcoding
  6. Untargeted LC–MS Metabolomics Workflow (metabolomics-msdial-workflow). Untargeted LC–MS/MS metabolomics workflow in MS-DIAL (KO vs WT, HILIC, ESI+): peak detection, alignment, spectral annotation and downstream statistics; completed training exercise, workflow and parameters only, no raw data, software or spectral libraries. Private
    https://github.com/BariSayed/metabolomics-msdial-workflow
